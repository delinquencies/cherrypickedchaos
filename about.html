About Us · Meet Melissa & Candy

Two generations.
One good kind of chaos.

Two different styles, one shared love of finding the good stuff.

Cherry Picked Chaos is our mother-daughter adventure: a vintage and thrift shop in Schulenburg, Texas, filled with personality, nostalgia, and plenty of surprises. We believe the best spaces and wardrobes are collected over time — and the best treasures have stories.

Melissa

The retro soul with a soft spot for the ’70s.

A counseling practice owner in Katy, a mom of three, and a lifelong treasure hunter, Melissa loves funky patterns, vintage clothing, and pieces with a little history and a lot of personality. Her favorite finds are the ones that deserve another chance to be loved.

Candy

The eye for collected, cozy, country charm.

Mom of two and proud grandmother of four, Candy brings a love of design and decorating to the mix. Colonial, Folk, and Country Kitsch are her sweet spot. If something has history, charm, or a little quirky personality, she already knows where it belongs.

Together, we love traveling, exploring, and planning the occasional trip around an antique store, estate sale, or roadside treasure. We’re finding our groove in Schulenburg, and meeting the people who wander through our doors has become one of our favorite parts.

Inside, you’ll find an eclectic mix of vintage clothing, home décor, collectibles, glassware, unique gifts, and those wonderfully random things you didn’t know you needed. Think curated, upcycled, repurposed, and reimagined — with room for the unexpected.

Coming from La Grange, Columbus, Flatonia, or Houston? Make us part of your next Schulenburg shopping day. Come dig around, stay awhile, and tell us what catches your eye.

A little vintage. A little funky. A little unexpected. And always a little chaos.
